## Description (1586/4000 chars)
Ready to fuel your body with the ultimate carnivore diet? The Carnivore Diet Guide is your one-stop app for everything meat! Whether you're just starting your journey or you’re already living the carnivore life, this app offers the tools and knowledge you need to succeed.

What You’ll Get:
- First 30 Days Program: Your first month on carnivore, day by day. Each day has a short lesson, one task and what's normal to expect, plus a quick check-in on your energy, digestion, sleep and cravings.
- See Your Progress: Charts show how you're changing week to week. With Carnivore+, add your own trackers, like sinuses, joints or skin.
- Daily Reminders: A morning heads-up on what to watch for, and an evening nudge to check in.
- Essential Guides: Step-by-step tips for beginners and advanced carnivores alike.
- Tasty Recipes: From juicy steaks to nutrient-packed organ meats, we’ve got you covered.
- Expert Articles: Learn about the health benefits, fitness tips, and more straight from the experts.
- Cited & Trustworthy: Articles backed by real sources, so you know where the guidance comes from.

Why The Carnivore Diet Guide?
- No carbs, no problem. We make it easy to stay on track and optimize your diet for peak health and performance.
- Simple, easy-to-follow tips and content tailored for both beginners and long-time carnivores.
- Week 1 of the program is free. Carnivore+ unlocks days 8 to 30, plus exclusive articles and premium recipes.

Download now and start your carnivore journey today!
